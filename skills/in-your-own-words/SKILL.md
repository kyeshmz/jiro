# In your own words

Read `references/shared-rules.md` from this skill's reported base directory.

Restate what you think the user's goals are and what problem they are trying to solve. Use plain language and your own wording. Preserve the user's constraints. Distinguish the desired outcome from a proposed implementation or a guessed cause.

State any material uncertainty as uncertainty. Do not add a new goal, start implementation, or turn a restatement request into a plan. This skill checks understanding of the user's request. `bro` instead simplifies the assistant's previous reply.
